ChatGPT SEO

The short answer

ChatGPT SEO is the practice of optimizing your brand so ChatGPT finds, trusts, and recommends it inside its answers. There is no ranked list of ten links — ChatGPT names a short set of trusted sources. Getting named is the goal, and the discipline behind it is Large Model Optimization (LLMO).

How ChatGPT SEO is different from Google SEO

Google SEO competes for a position on a list. ChatGPT SEO competes to be named in a single answer. When a user asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, there is no page two and often no links at all — just a few brands the model trusts enough to mention.

That changes the objective: instead of keywords and backlinks alone, you optimize for retrievability, trust, and repeatability — can a model find you, believe you, and restate what you do in one clean sentence?

How ChatGPT decides what to recommend

  • Training data: what the model learned about you from the public web — consistent, factual, widely-referenced brands win.
  • Live retrieval / browsing: when ChatGPT searches, well-structured pages with clear facts and schema are easier to pull and cite.
  • Entity trust: one consistent identity (name, description, links) everywhere tells the model you are a real, single authority.
  • Third-party validation: being mentioned on other reputable sites signals you’re worth recommending.

FAQ

Frequently asked

What is ChatGPT SEO?
ChatGPT SEO is optimizing your brand, content, and authority so ChatGPT retrieves, trusts, and recommends you in its answers. It’s a form of Large Model Optimization (LLMO) — the AI-era successor to traditional SEO.
Can you actually optimize for ChatGPT?
Yes. While you can’t directly edit ChatGPT, you can shape what it knows and trusts about you — through consistent factual content, structured data, a single clear identity across the web, and third-party mentions. Those are the inputs the model draws on.
Is ChatGPT SEO the same as LLMO?
Effectively yes. ChatGPT SEO is LLMO applied to ChatGPT specifically. The same discipline gets you cited by Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity too.
