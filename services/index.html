How it runs

Diagnosis, then construction, then compounding

Every engagement starts with a measured baseline — the twelve deliverables of the AI Authority Audit. Nothing gets built before the constraint is identified, because the most expensive marketing money is spent executing well against the wrong diagnosis.

Then the fixes, sequenced by leverage: entity contradictions and technical blockers first because they are fast and cheap, then content, then external authority, which is slowest to move. Then the same prompt set is re-run, so improvement is a measurement rather than a claim.

Questions

Before you get in touch

What do I actually receive from an engagement?

Named artifacts rather than activity reports. Depending on scope: the twelve-deliverable AI Authority Audit, a written diagnosis working backwards from your unit economics, a prioritized page plan drawn from your commercial prompt set, structured data and entity fixes, and a re-measured baseline showing what moved. Every service on this page lists what it produces.

How much does this cost?

Hourly consulting runs $350 to $450. Retainers and fractional CMO engagements are scoped to the work and quoted after a diagnostic, because pricing a plan before knowing the constraint is guesswork. What matters more than the rate is what a customer is worth to you — the same monthly figure is expensive for a business where an order is worth $60 and cheap where a client is worth $40,000.

Do I need a retainer?

Often not. A retainer is the right instrument when the work is genuinely continuous. If what you need is a one-time fix — an entity cleanup, a positioning decision, a technical problem — then an audit or a few hours of consulting will do it, and I will tell you that rather than sell you twelve months.

How quickly will I see results?

It depends which lever moves. Intake and conversion fixes affect revenue within weeks because the demand already exists and is being lost. AI visibility work typically shows movement in weeks as engines re-crawl and re-ground. Search authority operates over months and compounds. Any single timeline offered without that distinction is a sales figure.

Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?

Yes. The work is remote by default and clients span legal, medical and dental practices, ecommerce, hospitality, finance and textiles across the United States. Los Angeles is where I am based, not the limit of who I work with.
