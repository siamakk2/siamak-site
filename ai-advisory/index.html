Definition

What is an AI advisor?

An AI advisor is a senior, independent expert who guides a business owner or leadership team through artificial-intelligence decisions: which opportunities to pursue, which vendors to trust, what policies to set and how to measure results. Unlike a vendor or an agency, an AI advisor is paid for judgment, not for selling software or hours of execution.

In plain English

The experienced person you call before you sign the AI contract, and again when you want to know whether it's working.

Questions

AI Advisory: frequently asked questions

What does a fractional AI advisor do?

A fractional AI advisor gives you senior AI judgment for a few hours a month instead of a full-time hire: setting the AI roadmap, reviewing vendor proposals, writing your AI use policy, briefing leadership and checking whether the AI you've bought is paying off.

How is this different from your AI Advisor voice assistant?

The AI Advisor is a free AI you can talk to at any hour about growth and AI visibility. This page is about engaging me personally, as a human advisor, for decisions specific to your business.

How much of my time does it take?

Typically one standing session a week or fortnight, plus short questions in between. The baseline month asks a little more, mostly for access to people and numbers.

Can you advise our board or partners?

Yes. Quarterly briefings for boards, partners and leadership teams are a standard part of the engagement, written so non-technical directors can make decisions from them.

Is your AI use policy legal advice?

No. It's an operational policy written in plain English. For regulated businesses I recommend having your counsel review it, and I'm happy to work alongside them.

Do you recommend your own products?

Only when they're genuinely the best fit, and I'll say so plainly when they are. Most of the time the right answer is a tool you already own, a mainstream product or doing nothing yet.
