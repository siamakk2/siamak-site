Definition

What is AI consulting?

AI consulting is outside expertise that helps a business decide where artificial intelligence will create measurable value, choose the right tools, implement them safely and train people to use them. Good AI consulting starts with the business problem (revenue, cost, speed or risk) and treats the technology as the last decision, not the first.

In plain English

Someone who has done this before tells you what's worth doing with AI, what isn't, and then helps you do it.

Why AI projects stall

Most businesses don't have an AI problem. They have a sequencing problem.

The tools are cheap and everywhere. What's scarce is knowing which problem to point them at, in which order, and who owns the result once the demo is over.

◇

Tool-first buying

Subscriptions get bought before anyone has named the problem they're meant to solve. Six months later nobody can say what changed.

◎

Pilots that never graduate

A demo works in a meeting. Nobody owns turning it into something the team relies on, so it quietly dies.

⚡

Invisible to AI-led buyers

Customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews who to hire, and your business isn't in the answer.

◈

Teams left out of it

Staff either distrust the tools or use them quietly without guardrails. Both are risks you can't see.

How an engagement runs

Diagnose, prove, then scale

01

Diagnose

A free 30-minute call, then a baseline: your numbers, your workflows, the tools already in use, and how AI systems currently describe your business.

02

Prioritise

Every opportunity scored on value, effort, readiness and risk. Most businesses get a short list, and that's the point.

03

Pilot

One use case, a success metric agreed before work starts, and a timeline in weeks rather than quarters.

04

Implement

The production build: integrations, permissions, guardrails, documentation and training for the people who'll own it.

05

Measure & compound

A monthly review of what moved. When the first constraint is solved, we move to the next one.

Questions

AI Consulting: frequently asked questions

How much does AI consulting cost?

Hourly consulting runs $350–$450 an hour with no minimum, which suits specific questions and second opinions. The AI Authority Audit is a fixed $1,500. Larger work is scoped at a fixed price after a diagnosis, and continuous work runs on a monthly retainer. The first 30-minute strategy call is free, and you can book and pay for a full hour online. See services for all four engagement models.

What's the difference between this page and your AI consultant page?

The AI consultant page is about me: who I am and how I think. This page is the service catalogue: what AI consulting covers, how an engagement runs and what you receive.

Do we need technical staff to work with an AI consultant?

No. Most of my clients are owner-led businesses without an IT department. I handle the technical side and hand over working systems with plain-English documentation and training.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's rarely the goal, and rarely the outcome. I design AI to remove the repetitive part of jobs (re-typing, sorting, chasing, first drafts) so people spend their time on judgment, relationships and the work customers actually pay for.

Which AI tools and models do you use?

I'm model-agnostic. I work with OpenAI, Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini and open-source models, and I prefer connecting to the software you already pay for over adding new subscriptions. The tool is chosen per job.

Do you work outside Los Angeles?

Yes. I'm based in Los Angeles with a presence in Napa Valley, and most engagements run online with clients across the United States and abroad.
