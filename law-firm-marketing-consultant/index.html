For Law Firms

Your next client is asking AI which firm to call.

Most law firm marketing sells you traffic. Traffic is not the problem. The problem is that a prospect with a real case reads your page and does not pick up the phone — and increasingly, never reaches your page at all, because they asked an assistant for a recommendation and got three other names.

The diagnosis

Why firm websites don't convert

Practice-area pages that read like a brochure. No specificity about outcomes. Intake that assumes the visitor will fill in a form at 11pm on a Sunday. And an authority footprint too thin for a model to cite you with confidence.

▸Undifferentiated practice pages Every firm in your county says "aggressive representation." A prospect comparing four sites sees one site four times.
▸No proof at the decision point Case results, credentials and reviews live on a separate page nobody visits. They belong beside the call to action.
▸Intake friction A nine-field form is a filter. It filters out the people who were ready.
▸Thin entity signals Inconsistent firm name, address and attorney bios across directories. Models resolve ambiguity by citing someone else.

Discovery

How clients actually find a law firm through AI

Assistants and search engines behave differently, and the difference decides whether you are on the shortlist.

▸They describe a situation, not a practice area People do not search "employment litigation." They describe what happened at work and ask what their options are. The assistant classifies the matter and then names firms — so content organised around your practice-area taxonomy answers a question nobody asked.
▸The assistant answers the legal question first Most legal queries are informational, and the model answers them directly. The firm that gets named is the one whose published material was good enough to ground that answer. Being the source of the explanation is now the acquisition channel.
▸Jurisdiction is a hard filter Bar admissions, courts you appear in, counties served. Specific and checkable, and frequently missing from firm sites that say "serving clients nationwide" without stating where you are actually admitted.
▸Matter-type specificity wins A firm with real depth published on one matter type is named ahead of a general practice listing fifteen. Depth is legible to a model in a way that breadth is not.
Diagnostic

What an audit reveals for a law firm

The full twelve deliverables are on the AI Authority Audit page. Here is what they surface in this field specifically.

▸Attorney-level entity coherence Each attorney is an entity across state bar records, Martindale, Justia, Avvo, LinkedIn and your own bios. Firms contradict themselves constantly here, especially after lateral moves, and it undermines confidence in every claim about the firm.
▸What your reviews cause models to say Review substance shapes recommendation. Comments about responsiveness produce a different recommendation than comments about outcomes — and responsiveness is what most legal reviews actually discuss.
▸Which legal sources feed the answers Bar directories, legal publishers, court records and specific legal media carry disproportionate weight. The audit names which ones operate in your jurisdiction and matter types.
▸Advertising-compliance exposure State bar advertising rules vary and apply to what a model repeats about you as much as to what you publish. Existing claims that would be difficult to defend are flagged before they become a problem.
Conversion

From visibility to a signed matter

Legal intake fails on speed more than anything else. A prospective client with a live problem contacts several firms and retains whichever responds first with a human being. Firms lose matters to slower response times far more often than to weaker marketing, and the loss never appears in an analytics report.

▸Measure response time, not lead volume How long from inquiry to a human conversation, measured honestly including evenings and weekends. This number usually explains the conversion rate on its own.
▸Qualify before the consultation, not during it Publishing matter types you take, jurisdictions you cover, and how fees work filters out the inquiries that waste attorney time and raises the quality of the ones that arrive.
▸Make the first step concrete What the consultation costs, how long it takes, what to bring, what happens next. Vagueness at this step loses clients who were ready to retain.

The engagement

What an engagement asks of your team

Stated plainly, because the honest answer to "how much of my time will this take" is what most proposals avoid.

01

Weeks 1–2 · Diagnostic

Site, bar listings, attorney profiles and intake data. About an hour with whoever handles intake — the person who knows which inquiries convert and which waste time.

02

Weeks 3–6 · Foundations

Attorney entity cleanup across bar and directory records, jurisdiction and matter-type clarity, structured data, and a compliance review of existing claims. Administrative verification only.

03

Ongoing · Content

Roughly an hour a month of attorney time. Every substantive page is reviewed by an admitted attorney before publication, both for accuracy and for state bar advertising compliance. I draft; you correct. I will not publish material characterising outcomes or implying results that would not survive a bar review.

04

Quarterly · Review

Prompt set re-run against baseline, measured on signed matters by type rather than total inquiries.

Answers

Common questions

?How long before a law firm sees results from marketing? Meaningful movement in competitive legal markets takes three to six months, and durable authority takes twelve or more. New content typically takes days to weeks just to be discovered and indexed. Anyone promising first-page rankings in thirty days is either targeting queries nobody searches or is not being honest with you.
?Do you work with firms outside Los Angeles? Yes. The consultancy is based in Los Angeles and engagements run fully online, with clients nationwide.
?What makes AI visibility different from traditional SEO for law firms? Traditional search returns a list of links and the prospect chooses. An AI assistant returns one synthesized answer naming two or three firms. If your firm is not one of them, the prospect never sees you at all — there is no page two to rank on.
?Do you replace our existing marketing agency? Usually not. Most engagements diagnose whether the problem is the agency's execution or the strategy underneath it, then either fix the brief the agency is working from or recommend a change. Both outcomes are common.
