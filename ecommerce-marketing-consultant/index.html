The diagnosis

Where the revenue actually leaks

In most stores the leak is not the ad account. It is the gap between what the ad promised and what the landing page delivers, and a product catalogue no model can read.

▸Message mismatch The ad sold one thing, the page shows another. Bounce is the customer telling you they were misled, not that they weren't interested.
▸Thin product data Missing specs, no structured data, no comparison context. Shoppers can't decide and models can't recommend.
▸Trust gap at checkout Returns policy, shipping cost and reviews surfaced too late — after the customer has already decided to think about it.
▸Attribution fog Spend allocated on last-click data that credits the wrong channel and starves the one actually working.

Answers

Common questions

?Why does my store get traffic but no sales? Most often a mismatch between the promise that brought the visitor in and what the landing page delivers, combined with trust signals surfaced too late in the flow. It is a diagnosable problem, and it is rarely fixed by buying more traffic.
?Do you work with Shopify stores? Yes. I run my own Shopify stores and built the Custom Pattern Studio inside Blue Moon Fabrics, so the recommendations come from operating the platform rather than reading about it.
?How do I get my products recommended by AI assistants? Structured product data, genuine comparison content, and consistent entity signals across the web. Models recommend what they can retrieve, ground and attribute with confidence. Thin catalogue data makes that impossible regardless of how good the product is.
?What size store is this worth it for? Generally stores already spending meaningfully on acquisition, where a conversion-rate improvement pays for the engagement several times over. Below that, the honest answer is usually to fix the product page yourself first.
