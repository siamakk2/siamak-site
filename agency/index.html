Questions

Before you start

What does the done-for-you AI agency do?

Siamak Kalhor Consulting sets the strategy and does the work for you: AI visibility (getting your business named by ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity), AI-ready websites, AI agents and chatbots, and monthly content. You approve the work; you do not have to do it.

Do I need a sales call before I buy?

No. Each package has a fixed price and a checkout button. After you pay, you fill in a short brief about your business and we email a kickoff plan within one business day. You can still call 323-657-7752 if you would rather talk first.

Who actually does the work?

Siamak Kalhor, an AI consultant with 40 years in marketing, directs every project and reviews the work. Delivery uses AI tools and automation built in-house, which is how fixed prices stay this low without cutting corners.

What if my business is not a good fit?

We review every order within one business day. If we cannot do the work well for you, we tell you and refund the payment in full before any work starts.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes. Monthly plans run month to month. Email or call to cancel and billing stops at the end of the current month.

Which platforms do you work with?

Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and custom sites. For AI agents, we connect to your email, calendar or CRM where it has an integration.

Are you a full-service digital marketing agency?

In practice, yes, but consultant-led rather than a large firm. One accountable strategist, Siamak Kalhor, sets the direction and reviews every deliverable, and the packages cover AI visibility, AI-ready websites, AI agents and content. You buy at a fixed price online instead of signing a long retainer.

What outsourced marketing services can a small business buy here?

AI Visibility Launch ($2,500 one-time), AI Visibility Growth ($1,500 a month), AI-Ready Website (from $4,500), a Done-For-You AI Agent ($3,500 setup plus $1,000 a month) and the Content Engine ($1,200 a month). For strategy on its own, a paid strategy hour is $350.

Is this an integrated marketing agency?

Yes, in the sense that matters: your website, search and AI visibility, content and follow-up are planned as one system against one set of numbers, not bought as separate channels that never talk to each other.
