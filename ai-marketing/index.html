Definition

What is AI marketing?

AI marketing is the use of artificial intelligence across the marketing function (research, segmentation, content creation, advertising, personalization and measurement) to make decisions faster and execute at a scale a small team couldn't reach alone. It also means marketing to buyers whose research now runs through AI assistants, which changes how brands get discovered and chosen.

In plain English

Using AI to do better marketing, and doing marketing that still works when your customers ask an AI first.

The trade-off

Where AI makes marketing better, and where it quietly makes it worse

The same tool that writes a month of posts in an afternoon can also erase everything that made your brand recognisable. The difference is the system around it.

⚡

Where AI helps

Research, first drafts, variations for testing, audience analysis, ad iteration and reporting: speed and volume a small team never had.

◈

Where AI hurts

Sameness. When everyone prompts the same tools, the output converges. Generic content is invisible to people and to AI systems alike.

◇

Where it's risky

Confident errors: invented facts, claims you can't support and messages that drift off-brand without anyone noticing.

◎

What fixes it

Positioning first, a written voice system, human editing on everything public and measurement tied to revenue.

Questions

AI Marketing: frequently asked questions

What does an AI marketing consultant do?

An AI marketing consultant decides where AI belongs in your marketing (research, content, advertising, personalization, measurement), sets up the systems and guardrails, and makes sure the result produces customers rather than just more content. The strategy is the job; the tools are the means.

Will AI-written content hurt my SEO?

Not because AI wrote it. Google's guidance is that it rewards helpful, reliable content however it's produced, while mass-produced, low-value content made to manipulate rankings breaks its spam policies. The risk is generic content, not the tool.

Do I still need a marketing agency?

Sometimes. If you need production capacity across many channels, an agency can provide it. What AI changes is that small teams can now do far more themselves, so many businesses need senior strategy and a good system more than they need another retainer.

Which AI marketing tools should we use?

Fewer than you'd think. Most businesses get the bulk of the value from one general-purpose AI assistant, the AI features already inside their ad platforms and CRM, and a good analytics setup. I recommend tools after the strategy, never before.

How do you keep AI content on-brand?

With a written voice system (a guide, approved examples and tuned prompts) and a rule that a person edits and approves everything public. That's what keeps a brand distinct when everyone has the same tools.
