Definition

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is search engine optimization for an era when AI systems answer the query. The term has two meanings: using AI tools to do SEO work faster, and optimizing a website so that AI-powered search (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Gemini and Perplexity) can find, trust and cite it. The second meaning matters more, because it decides whether your business appears in the answer at all.

In plain English

Making sure that when someone asks a search engine or an AI about what you do, your business is part of the answer.

Two meanings

“AI SEO” means two things. Only one is an advantage.

When an agency says it does AI SEO, ask which one it means. Both are useful; only one is hard to copy.

◇

Meaning 1: AI for SEO

Using AI to research keywords, cluster topics, draft outlines and run audits. Faster, but everyone has the same tools, so it's quickly becoming table stakes.

⚡

Meaning 2: SEO for AI

Making your business something AI systems can find, verify and quote. That's where visibility is won or lost now, and it compounds.

◈

The shared foundation

Both rest on the same base: a crawlable site, a clear identity, structured data and facts that other sources confirm.

◎

Why it's urgent

When the answer appears above the links, being ranked fourth may mean never being seen. Being cited in the answer is the new first position.

Questions

AI SEO: frequently asked questions

Is AI SEO the same as LLMO, GEO or AEO?

They overlap heavily. Large Language Model Optimization (LLMO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) all describe the “SEO for AI” half of AI SEO. AI SEO is the broader label that also includes classic Google SEO. See SEO vs. LLMO for the full comparison.

Does traditional SEO still matter?

Yes. AI search features retrieve from web indexes, so pages that can't be crawled, indexed and trusted rarely get cited. AI SEO builds on classic SEO; it doesn't replace it.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT or Google will recommend my business?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. No one controls these systems. What I can do is remove what's blocking you, make your business unambiguous and well-corroborated, and measure the change honestly.

Should I block AI crawlers?

It depends on which ones. Crawlers that power AI search (OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot, for example) are how your pages get cited in answers. Crawlers used for model training, such as GPTBot, are a separate choice. Blocking everything can remove you from AI answers entirely.

How do you measure AI SEO results?

Two ways: Search Console for classic search, and a fixed set of real buyer questions run monthly across the major AI assistants, tracking whether and how your business is named.

How long does AI SEO take?

Technical fixes can show up within weeks of being recrawled. Entity clarity and external corroboration take months, because they depend on other sources and on systems updating. Plan in quarters, measure monthly.
