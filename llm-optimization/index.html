FAQ

Common questions

What is LLM Optimization?

LLM Optimization (also called Large Model Optimization or LLMO) is the practice of structuring your brand's information, authority, and digital footprint so large language models like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude cite and recommend you. It's the AI-era successor to SEO.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO optimizes for ranked links on a search page. LLM Optimization optimizes for being the synthesized, cited answer an AI generates. SEO targets crawlers and rankings; LLMO targets retrieval, grounding, and attribution inside the model.

How long does it take to see results?

Foundational signals — schema, entity consistency, structured content — can be in place within weeks. Authority and citation frequency compound over months as models re-ingest the web and learn to trust your brand as a source.
