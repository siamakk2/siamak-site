Questions

What buyers and tenants ask.

How long has K2 Investment been in business?

Since 1994 — more than thirty years of continuous operation in Downtown Los Angeles. That span covers the dot-com cycle, the 2008 financial crisis, the DTLA revival of the 2010s, the COVID era and the current market reset. Very few boutique commercial firms in Los Angeles have transacted through all of them.

What areas does K2 cover?

Downtown Los Angeles is the firm's home market — specifically the Fashion, Flower and Garment Districts along Santee Street, S Los Angeles Street, and E 8th and E 9th Streets. K2 also represents clients in Napa Valley for estate, land and vineyard property.

What services does K2 provide?

Three: sales and acquisition, leasing (both landlord and tenant representation), and property management. The firm handles commercial property throughout DTLA and residential and land transactions in Napa Valley.

Who runs the firm?

Bobak Kalhor founded K2 Investment, Inc. in 1994 and is the owner and broker of record, CA DRE #01201662. His brother Siamak Kalhor, CA DRE #00908799, works alongside him and leads the Napa Valley side of the business. The two operate as partners across the firm's two markets.

What types of buyers does K2 represent?

1031 exchange buyers, owner-users, international purchasers and private capital on the commercial side. In Napa, estate and land buyers who need genuine due diligence on acreage, water, zoning and county requirements.

Does K2 handle off-market transactions?

Yes. Thirty years in the same corridors means the firm often knows which owners are considering a sale before a property is listed. A meaningful share of K2's transaction history never reached the open market.

Where is K2 Investment located?

530 E 8th Street, Suite 400, Los Angeles, CA 90014 — in the Flower District, in the middle of the corridors the firm has transacted since 1994.
