The Shift

SEO got you ranked. LLMO gets you cited.

A growing share of buyers now ask an AI before they ask Google. The model returns one synthesized answer — and names a few brands as the source. If you're not one of them, you're invisible at the exact moment of intent. This is the discipline that fixes that.

01

Retrieval, not ranking

Models don't show ten blue links. They retrieve, synthesize, and attribute. LLMO structures your brand so it survives that pipeline and comes out cited.

02

Trust is engineered

Consistent entities, structured data, and authoritative citations are the signals models weigh. We build them deliberately — not by accident.

03

Authority compounds

Once a model learns to trust your brand as a source, it keeps citing you. Early movers compound an advantage that's expensive to dislodge.
