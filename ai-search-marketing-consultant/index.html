AI Search Marketing

AI Search Marketing
Consultant

An AI search marketing consultant makes a business retrievable and citable by AI assistants — so that when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity for a business like yours, your name is in the answer. It is the same job people call LLMO, GEO, AEO or AI SEO. The terminology is unsettled; the work is not.

For the concept rather than the service, start with AI visibility.

The words are a mess. Here is the map.

Five terms are circulating for one discipline, and every vendor prefers whichever one they coined or adopted first.

LLMO — Large Language Model Optimization. Making a brand legible to language models.
GEO — Generative Engine Optimization. Same goal, framed around generative answers.
AEO — Answer Engine Optimization. Same goal, framed around answers rather than links.
AI SEO — usually traditional SEO with AI tooling bolted on, which is a different thing.
AI visibility — the plain-language version, and the one clients actually use.

The first three describe the same work. Anyone insisting on a hard distinction is usually defending a trademark rather than explaining a method.

You do not need to know which acronym is winning. You need to know whether AI mentions your business.

Why isn't ChatGPT recommending my business?

Almost always because the evidence about you is thinner or less consistent than the evidence about a competitor — not because their product is better.

An assistant builds an answer from sources that agree with each other. Your own website is one claim, and the one a model trusts least, because it is you describing yourself. What gives it weight is corroboration: a directory listing with the same address, a review platform with the same phone number, a trade publication that quoted you, an association page that lists you.

When those agree, the model has a fact. When they conflict — an old suite number here, a disconnected number there, a business name that shortened three years ago but not everywhere — the model has ambiguity, and it resolves ambiguity by naming a business it is surer about.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

In the order that actually matters:

Make your facts identical everywhere. Name, address, phone, category. Not similar — identical, down to whether you write Suite, Ste or #. This is dull and it outperforms everything else, because inconsistency is the commonest reason a business gets skipped.

Get corroborated by sources you do not own. The directories and review platforms your industry actually uses. Absence from them is not a gap in your marketing, it is a gap in the evidence a model has to work with.

State your facts in a form a machine cannot misread. Structured data describing what you do, where, and who you are. Not a ranking trick — the difference between a model inferring and knowing.

Answer the questions people actually ask, in their words, with the answer in the first two sentences rather than the eighth paragraph. Models quote what is quotable.

Then measure it. Not rankings — presence. Ask the assistants what your customers ask and see whether your name comes back. Most businesses have never checked once.

What the work involves

Entity definition, so every system agrees on who you are and what you do. Consistency auditing across every place your business appears. Structured data that states your facts unambiguously. Content built to answer real questions directly. Corroboration — getting cited by sources that do not belong to you. Common questions

What is an AI search marketing consultant?
An AI search marketing consultant makes a business retrievable and citable by AI assistants, so that when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity for a business like theirs, that business is named in the answer. It is the same discipline others call LLMO, GEO or AEO.

Why isn't ChatGPT recommending my business?
Usually because the evidence about your business is thinner or less consistent than the evidence about a competitor. Assistants build answers from sources that corroborate each other. If your name, address or phone number differs across listings, or you are missing from the platforms the assistant reads, it resolves that ambiguity by naming a business it is more confident about.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?
Make your name, address, phone and category identical everywhere. Get listed on the directories and review platforms your industry actually uses. Add structured data stating plainly what you do and where. Answer real customer questions in the customer's words, with the answer in the opening sentences. Then measure whether assistants name you, per assistant, because they disagree.

What is the difference between LLMO, GEO and AEO?
Very little. LLMO is Large Language Model Optimization, GEO is Generative Engine Optimization, AEO is Answer Engine Optimization. All three describe making a business appear in AI-generated answers. AI SEO usually means traditional SEO with AI tooling, which is a different thing. Anyone insisting on a hard distinction between the first three is generally defending terminology rather than explaining a method.

Can you guarantee my business will appear in ChatGPT?
No, and nobody can. No one controls what an AI assistant recommends. What can be influenced is the evidence those systems read: consistency, corroboration, structure and relevance. Any consultant guaranteeing placement in AI answers is either mistaken or being dishonest.
