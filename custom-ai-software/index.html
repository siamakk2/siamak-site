Definition

What is custom AI software development?

Custom AI software development is designing and building software that uses artificial intelligence for one business's specific workflows, data and customers, rather than adapting a generic tool. It typically includes AI assistants and agents, document and data processing, voice interfaces and integrations with the systems the business already uses.

In plain English

Software made for your business, with AI doing the reading, sorting, answering and drafting your team does by hand today.

When off-the-shelf falls short

Generic AI tools are cheap. Fitting them to your business isn't.

Custom software is worth it when one of these is true.

◇

Your workflow is your edge

The way you quote, schedule or serve customers is what makes you better, and no tool does it your way.

◎

Your data lives in five places

Spreadsheets, inboxes, an old system and someone's memory. AI can't help until it's connected.

⚡

Customers need answers now

Questions arrive at all hours, in several languages, and every slow answer is a lost sale.

◈

Subscriptions stacking up

You pay for several tools that each do a fraction of the job and don't talk to each other.

Questions

Custom AI Software: frequently asked questions

What does custom AI software development cost for a small business?

It depends on scope, which is why every build gets a written fixed-price scope first. Some builds have published prices: an AI agent or chatbot is $3,500 setup plus $1,000 a month, and an AI-Ready Website starts at $4,500. See pricing.

What AI solutions work best for small businesses?

The ones tied to a job done many times a week: answering and following up with leads, quoting, scheduling, processing documents, and answering customer questions. Start where hours or lost sales are easiest to measure. My guide Which Parts of My Business Should I Automate First? helps you choose.

Do you build with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

All of them, plus open-source models. I'm model-agnostic and choose per job, based on quality, cost, privacy and how it connects to your systems.

Who owns the software and the data?

You do. Your data stays yours, and systems are built with encryption, access control and consent-first design. See my approach to responsible AI.

Can you connect AI to the software we already use?

Usually, yes. Connecting to your existing tools is often better value than adding new ones, and it's where most of the time savings come from.

How long does a build take?

A prototype typically takes weeks, not months. The full timeline is in the written scope before you commit.
