What does an AI business consultant actually do?

Siamak KalhorForty years in marketing — 5 minute read

Owners ask me this more than almost anything else now: what does an AI business consultant actually do, and how is that different from buying a subscription and figuring it out?

Fair question. The title is new, the market is crowded, and plenty of people calling themselves AI consultants learned the tools last spring. So here is what the job should be, what it shouldn't be, and how to tell the difference before you pay anyone.

The job, in plain terms

A good AI business consultant looks at the whole business, not one tool. The work has four parts.

Find the leaks. Where does time or money disappear? Missed calls. Enquiries answered the next day. Quotes that never get a follow-up. Reports someone rebuilds by hand every Monday. These are the places AI pays for itself first.

Put them in order. Not everything should happen at once. The first project should be small, measurable and close to revenue, so you can see whether it worked within weeks, not quarters.

Set the rules. What may the AI see? What may it do on its own? When does a person step in? What happens to customer data? Those decisions are the consultant's job, not the software vendor's.

Say no. This is the part most people skip. A consultant who has never told a client "don't use AI for that" is a salesperson with a new title.

AI business consultant, AI strategy advisor, AI marketing consultant

The names overlap, and buyers search all three. Here is how I'd separate them.

Three titles, three different scopes
TitleLooks atTypical first deliverable
AI business consultantThe whole operation: calls, intake, sales, operations, reportingA ranked list of where AI saves time or money, with a first pilot
AI strategy advisorDecisions: what to adopt, in what order, at what cost, with which safeguardsA short plan and someone to call when the next decision comes up
AI marketing consultantMarketing: AI search visibility, content, follow-up, measurementAn AI visibility baseline and a marketing plan that uses AI where it pays

In a small business, one experienced person often covers all three. That's how I work: AI consulting for the business, AI advisory for ongoing decisions, and AI marketing for the part of the business I've spent forty years in.

What it should not look like

Three warning signs, all of which I've seen in proposals owners have brought to me.

A tool list instead of a plan. "You should be using these twelve AI tools" is a shopping list. A plan names the problem first and the tool second, and usually involves far fewer tools.

No measure of success. If the proposal can't say what number should move, nobody will know whether the money was well spent. Hours saved, enquiries answered within five minutes, quotes followed up. Something you can count.

Everything at once. Ten projects launched in month one usually means ten half-finished projects in month six. Start with one.

How to pick one

Ask four questions on the first call.

What would you not use AI for in my business? If the answer is "nothing," keep looking.

What have you built or run yourself? Advice is cheap. Ask to see something live. I point people to my own AI agents and the apps on my portfolio because I'd rather be judged on what runs than on what I say.

How will we know it worked? You want a number and a date.

What happens to our customer data? A clear answer here tells you more about competence than any case study.

Common questions

What does an AI business consultant do?
An AI business consultant looks across the whole business, finds the places where AI saves real time or money, such as answering calls, intake, follow-up, quoting and reporting, puts them in order and sets the rules for using AI safely. A good one also tells you where AI isn't worth it yet.

What is an AI strategy advisor?
An AI strategy advisor is a senior adviser an owner can call on for AI decisions without hiring a full-time technology executive: which tools to adopt, what to automate first, what to avoid and how to measure it. The value is judgment at the moment a decision has to be made.

Is an AI marketing consultant the same as an AI business consultant?
Not quite. An AI marketing consultant focuses on marketing: being found and recommended in AI answers, producing content faster and automating follow-up. An AI business consultant looks at operations too. In a small business, one experienced person often covers both.

How much does an AI business consultant cost?
Rates vary widely. Mine are published: a free 30-minute first call, a $350 strategy hour and $350 to $450 an hour after that, with larger engagements quoted after a diagnostic.

How do I know if an AI consultant is any good?
Ask what they would not use AI for in your business, what they have built and run themselves, how success will be measured and what happens to your customer data. Vague answers to any of those are a reason to keep looking.
