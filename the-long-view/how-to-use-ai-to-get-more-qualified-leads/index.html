How to use AI to get more qualified leads, not just more leads.

Siamak KalhorForty years in marketing — 7 minute read

Owners don't ask me for "more leads" anymore. They ask for better ones. They have tried buying volume, and they have learned that fifty enquiries from the wrong people cost more time than they're worth.

AI can help with both halves of the problem: being found by the right buyers, and recognising them quickly when they arrive. Here is how I'd use it, in order.

Step zero: decide who a qualified lead is

This is strategy, not technology, and skipping it is why most "AI lead generation" disappoints. Write down, in a sentence, the customer you want more of: their situation, their problem, their budget, where they are. Every step below depends on that sentence. You can't optimize your way out of a bad strategy.

1. Be found where qualified buyers now ask

The most qualified buyers increasingly skip the list of links. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI "who's the best person for my exact situation?" and they get two or three names. If you're one of them, the lead arrives already half-sold.

Getting named takes four things:

  • Consistent facts. Your name, services, location and people described the same way everywhere, so AI is sure you're one business.
  • Answer-first pages. Pages that answer the questions your ideal customer asks, specifically enough that AI can quote them.
  • Third-party proof. Reviews, directories, press and expert mentions that AI uses to decide who to trust.
  • Measurement. The same questions asked of the AI tools every month, so you can see whether it's working.

This discipline goes by several names (GEO, AEO and LLMO) and I explain it on GEO & AEO consulting. The important part is that it attracts buyers who already match what you do, because that's the question they asked.

2. Answer and qualify every enquiry instantly

A lead is most qualified in the first few minutes after it contacts you. Research published in Harvard Business Review found that firms contacting a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as firms that waited longer. An AI assistant can reply in seconds at any hour, ask the three or four questions that separate a good fit from a poor one, and route the good ones straight to your calendar.

Qualification also protects your time. The assistant can politely point the poor fits elsewhere, so your people spend their hours on the conversations that matter.

3. Follow up until they decide

Many qualified buyers simply aren't ready this week. Without follow-up, they quietly become someone else's customer. AI can keep a helpful, personal sequence going (answers to the next question they'll have, a case study, a reminder) and alert a person the moment they re-engage. See Can AI follow up with leads automatically?

4. Measure leads by what they become

Count customers and revenue by source, not enquiries. A channel that sends ten enquiries and four clients beats one that sends a hundred and none. AI can tag and summarise every conversation so this reporting happens on its own.

What doesn't work

  • Buying lists and blasting AI-written cold emails. Volume without fit, and a fast way to damage your domain and your name.
  • Hundreds of thin AI-generated pages. They rarely rank, and they make your business harder for AI systems to understand.
  • Chatbots that trap people. Always offer a human. A frustrated qualified buyer is worse than no chatbot at all.

Common questions

Can AI generate leads for my business?
AI is better at helping qualified buyers find and choose you than at manufacturing leads from nothing. Its strongest uses are getting your business recommended in AI answers, responding instantly and following up consistently.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?
Make your business easy to identify, verify and quote: consistent facts across the web, structured data, pages that answer buyers' questions directly, and reviews and mentions on sites AI trusts.

What is a qualified lead?
A qualified lead is an enquiry from someone who has the problem you solve, can afford your solution, and is in a position to decide. Defining it in writing is the first step of any lead-generation plan.

How quickly will this work?
Instant response and follow-up can show results within weeks, because they recover leads you already have. Being recommended by AI tools builds over months, and compounds.
