Social Media Marketing

Social media marketing that builds brand and sells

Where discovery happens now — strategy, content, community, and paid social that turn scrolls into customers.

The short version

I run social media that does two jobs at once: build a brand people trust and drive measurable sales. Strategy, content, community, and paid — tied to the rest of your funnel, from someone who runs his own brands' social.

The problem: discovery moved to the feed

For huge categories — fashion, food, home, lifestyle — buyers discover brands while scrolling, not searching. Most businesses post inconsistently with no strategy, chase follower counts that don't convert, and never turn attention into sales.

What I do

Strategy — the platforms, cadence, and positioning that fit your brand and where your buyers actually are.
Content that stops the scroll — built to be shared and saved, not ignored.
Community & engagement — turning followers into a brand people trust and buy from.
Paid social — amplifying what works with targeted spend that ties back to sales.

Why work with me

I run social for my own brands and e-commerce stores, so I know what converts versus what just looks busy. It plugs straight into the rest of your funnel — internet marketing and brand creation — instead of running as a disconnected feed.
