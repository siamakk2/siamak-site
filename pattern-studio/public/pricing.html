Questions

Can I try before paying?

Yes — draft any garment, see the croquis and three photoreal renders on the free plan. Pro only adds exports, size runs, unlimited renders and cloud projects.

Do I own the patterns?

Yes. Everything you draft and export is yours to sew, sell or commission.

How do I cancel?

Open your account menu → Manage billing → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid month.

Is fabric included?

No — fabric is ordered separately from Blue Moon Fabrics; the studio adds the right yardage to their cart.
