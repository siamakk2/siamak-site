Cosplay

Built for cosplay. Fitted like couture.

Spandex bodysuits, dance leotards, boned corsets, costume bases — drafted to the body that will wear them, man or woman, then rendered in the exact fabric so you know before you cut.

Bodysuits & unitards

Full-length, tank, long-sleeve and zip-front catsuits with gusset and crotch pieces. Negative ease for compression fits.

Corsets & crop tops

Sweetheart and straight-neck corset tops with boning channels; princess-seam crop tops; sports-bra bases for armour.

Costume bases

Leotard and costume blocks ready for armour, capes and appliqué. Export DXF to a cutter or SVG to print at home.

Men’s and women’s models

Switch the model with one tap. Renders describe a men’s cut and chest sizing, or a women’s block, from the same drafted pattern.

Foil, sequin, velvet, mesh

Fabric behaviour changes the draft: nap direction for velvet, no-stretch allowances for foil, mesh insert panels.

See it before the con

Photoreal renders on a cosplay-booth or runway backdrop. Download and share the look for approval or commissions.
