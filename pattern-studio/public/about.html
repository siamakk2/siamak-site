About

Forty years of fabric. One new tool.

Pattern Studio started as the Blue Moon Fabrics pattern maker — a tool built inside a Shopify store so customers could draft a pattern and buy the right yardage in one place. It outgrew the store. This standalone studio keeps the proven drafting engine (24 garments, 90 styles) and adds what a store page could not: photoreal renders in the real fabric, men’s and women’s models, deep links, and a roadmap that is not limited by a theme editor.

It is built by Siamak Kalhor Consulting in Los Angeles, with Blue Moon Fabrics supplying the live catalogue and fulfilment. Blue Moon has served the LA Fashion District’s designers, dancewear makers and swimwear labels for decades; the studio is the digital version of leaning on the counter and asking “what would this look like in that?”

Questions, feature requests or press: siamakconsulting.com/contact.
