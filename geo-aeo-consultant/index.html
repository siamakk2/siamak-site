Definition

What are GEO, AEO and LLMO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Large Language Model Optimization (LLMO) are overlapping names for the practice of making a business visible, accurately described and recommended in AI-generated answers from tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews. The work combines entity consistency, structured data, answer-first content and third-party citations.

Questions

GEO & AEO: frequently asked questions

What is a GEO consultant?

A GEO consultant helps a business appear and be cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. The work covers entity consistency, structured data, answer-first content, third-party citations and monthly measurement of what AI says about the business.

What's the difference between GEO and AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) began with featured snippets and voice assistants: being the single direct answer. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is about being cited when an AI writes a new answer. In practice the same work serves both, which is why I treat them, with LLMO, as one discipline.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT?

Make it easy for AI to identify you, verify you and quote you: consistent business facts everywhere, structured data, pages that answer buyers' questions directly, and reviews and mentions on sites AI trusts. My guide How to Get Cited by AI covers the steps.

Why do my competitors show up in AI searches and I don't?

Usually because AI has more consistent, verifiable information about them: clearer entity facts, more citations, and content that answers the question being asked. The AI Authority Audit shows exactly where the gap is.

How much do AI search optimization services cost?

The AI Authority Audit is a fixed $1,500. Done-for-you packages start at $2,500 for an AI Visibility Launch, with ongoing AI Visibility Growth at $1,500 a month. See pricing.

What should I ask a GEO consultant before hiring one?

Ask how they measure your AI visibility, how they work on sources beyond your website, how they make content retrievable, how they diagnose a wrong AI answer and how they protect your Google traffic. I answer all five in public in Questions to Ask a GEO Consultant.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. AI systems still draw heavily on the open web, and Google's AI Overviews sit on top of Google's index. Good SEO is the foundation; GEO and AEO build on it. See SEO vs LLMO.
