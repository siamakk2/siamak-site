The problem: shoppers ask AI what to buy now

Product discovery has moved. Buyers ask ChatGPT for "the best [product] for X," pull up recommendations in Google's AI overviews, and discover brands through social feeds long before they hit your product page. If the models and platforms don't know your store — or describe it wrong — you're invisible at the exact moment someone's ready to buy.
