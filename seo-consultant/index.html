Definition

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant improves how a business is found in search. The work covers technical health (speed, crawling and structured data), local search (Google Business Profile, reviews and directories), content that answers what customers search for, and authority from links and mentions. A modern SEO consultant also covers AI search, known as generative engine optimization (GEO), answer engine optimization (AEO) or Large Language Model Optimization (LLMO), so the business is cited in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews.

In plain English

Someone who makes sure people find you when they search, whether they search on Google or ask an AI.

Questions

SEO Consultant: frequently asked questions

What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant finds out why a business isn't being found in search and fixes it: technical problems that stop Google reading the site, weak local listings, content that doesn't answer what customers search for, and a lack of trusted links and mentions. I also cover AI search, so ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews name the business too.

Should I hire an SEO consultant or an SEO agency?

Hire a consultant when you need to know what's actually wrong and what to fix first. Hire an agency when the plan is clear and you need a team to produce the work. Many businesses do both: the consultant sets the plan and checks the agency's work against enquiries, not activity.

What is an AI search optimization agency?

An AI search optimization agency works on getting businesses mentioned and recommended in AI answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google's AI Overviews. The work is also called GEO, AEO or LLMO. My done-for-you packages start at $2,500 for an AI Visibility Launch, with AI Visibility Growth at $1,500 a month.

What are GEO and AEO services?

GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your business cited when an AI writes an answer. AEO (answer engine optimization) makes your business the direct answer in featured snippets, voice search and AI answers. The same work serves both. See GEO & AEO.

What does an LLMO consultant do?

An LLMO consultant works on Large Language Model Optimization: making sure AI models identify your business correctly, describe it accurately and recommend it for the right questions. LLMO is my umbrella term for GEO and AEO, explained in full on LLM Optimization and in how to choose an LLMO consultant.

How much does an SEO consultant cost?

My first 30-minute call is free and hourly consulting is $350 to $450. The AI Authority Audit, which covers Google and AI search, is a fixed $1,500. Ongoing work is quoted after the audit or bought as a fixed-price package. See pricing.

Is SEO still worth it now that people use ChatGPT?

Yes. AI systems draw heavily on the open web, and Google's AI Overviews sit on top of Google's own index, so good SEO is still the foundation AI search is built on. What changes is the goal: being the business the answer names, not just a link on page one. See SEO vs LLMO.
