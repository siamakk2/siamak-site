The Vision

AisleBird solves a universal shopping problem: people spend time looking for items instead of shopping efficiently. The app combines three technologies to eliminate friction:

1. Voice Input — Say what you need naturally. "Add two cartons of almond milk" understands quantities and variations instantly.

2. LLM-Powered Categorization — An intelligent engine maps items to store aisles in real time. One item ("bananas") might go to Produce. Another ("banana bread mix") routes to Baking. Each of the 11 supported retail chains has different aisle structures — the system learns and adapts.

3. Geofencing — See nearby stores by distance. Walk through the store, tap your current aisle, and your list reorders to match your physical path.

Result: A fast, intuitive shopping experience that works across Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, Target, Walmart, CVS, Walgreens, Costco, Home Depot, Lowe's, Friedman's, and Tractor Supply — with one app.

Technology Stack

Frontend React-based responsive web app. Mobile-first design with home screen icon support.
Voice Recognition Web Speech API with real-time transcription and error handling for robust voice input.
AI/LLM Integration Large Language Models for intelligent keyword-to-category mapping. Learns 11 distinct retail formats.
Geofencing Geolocation API with Haversine distance calculation. Shows stores sorted by proximity in miles.
Store Database 235+ locations with verified coordinates across 11 retail chains. Real-time expansion infrastructure.
Deployment Vercel serverless deployment with instant auto-deploys. CDN delivery, 30-second push-to-live cycle.
